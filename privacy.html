PRIVACY POLICY.

EFFECTIVE 26 AUGUST 2026 // PHASE SHIFT STUDIO // ABN 47 149 923 249

1. About this policy

Phase Shift Studio respects your privacy and explains here how personal information is collected, used, stored and disclosed when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, request or accept a quote, make a payment or work with us.

2. Information we collect

We may collect your name, business name, email address, phone number, website address, project requirements, budget, correspondence, quote and acceptance records, invoice and payment status, and information you choose to provide during a project.

Our payment provider, Stripe, processes payment-card details. Phase Shift Studio does not receive or store your complete card number.

3. How information is collected

Information may be collected through our website enquiry form, email, telephone, meetings, quote-acceptance pages, invoices, payment services and ordinary project communications. Basic technical information may also be recorded by our website host for security and operation.

4. Why we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare quotes.
  • To provide, manage and support client projects.
  • To process payments, issue invoices and maintain business records.
  • To communicate about scope, approvals, delivery and support.
  • To protect our systems, prevent misuse and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information.

5. Service providers and overseas processing

We use service providers including Web3Forms for enquiries, Supabase for quote and project data, Stripe for payments and invoices, Resend for transactional email, and GitHub Pages for website hosting. These providers may store or process information outside Australia, including in the United States or other locations in which they or their infrastructure providers operate.

6. Storage and security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to a small digital studio to protect information against misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access. No internet or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably required to provide services, maintain project and payment records, resolve disputes and meet legal, accounting and tax obligations. Information that is no longer required is deleted or de-identified where reasonably practicable.

8. Access and correction

You may request access to personal information we hold about you or ask us to correct inaccurate information by contacting hello@phaseshiftstudio.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. Privacy questions and complaints

Please send privacy questions or complaints to hello@phaseshiftstudio.com. We will acknowledge and investigate a complaint within a reasonable period. If applicable, you may also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

10. Updates

We may update this policy when our services or information-handling practices change. The current version and effective date will remain available on this website.
