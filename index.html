        [ ORIGIN + APPROACH ]

        HOW PHASE SHIFT STARTED

        Phase Shift Studio grew from first-hand experience with apps, programs and business systems that added friction to work that should have felt simple.

        That experience led me to build tools differently: around the people doing the work, the information they need and the steps that genuinely help them move forward.

        EFFICIENCY IS NOT THE RESULT OF ADDITION — IT'S SUBTRACTION.

        Speed is distance divided by time. In business, time and workload are always competing—so Phase Shift works to shorten the distance: fewer steps to complete each task, more time for the product, service and people that matter.
